Commission Info

How it works

  1. Check the calendar for an open slot.
  2. Send a request with the commission type, character details, and references.
  3. Wait for my approval. Your request stays pending until then.
  4. Pay the down payment. I then send you a rough sketch.
  5. Approve the sketch. I finish the lines, colors, and shading.
  6. Receive your final art and request revisions.

Frequently asked questions

What do I draw?

Original characters, fan art, portraits, and chibis. And can go to any artstyles.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds at the sketch stage. After I start coloring, only small fixes are free. Big changes may cost more.

How long does a commission take?

Most pieces take 3 to 7 days. Full-body art and detailed backgrounds take longer, so send your deadline early.

How do I pay?

A down payment is needed before I start the sketch. The balance is paid before I send the final files. Money transfer is through Gcash.

Can I use the art commercially?

Personal use is the default. Please tell me your plans in the request, because commercial use has a different rate. I may show the finished art in my portfolio.
